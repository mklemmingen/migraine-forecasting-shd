Why blended_xgb_lr_spano2026 appears only at the canonical 70_15_15 / chrono cell

The architecture's held-out calibration set drives four sequential optimisation steps: per-base isotonic + Platt calibrators, alpha grid search, final-calibrator selection, and downstream operating-threshold selection. Because the isotonic calibrators effectively memorise the calibration set, threshold-derived metrics (MCC, Sensitivity ≥ 0.5, F1) on test are unreliable; AUROC and AUPRC remain trustworthy because they are rank-based and calibration-invariant.

The architecture is preserved as a faithful replication of the prior bachelor-thesis baseline (Spano 2026, single operating point). Fanning it out across ratios and split types would add cells whose threshold metrics could not be cleanly compared. The methodologically clean comparator is stacked_2xgb_meta_lr, which uses two-parameter Platt calibration only and is fanned out to the full grid.
